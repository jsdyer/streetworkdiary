Sources and interpretation

The Halifax Cityworks service-request table records administrative status, closure dates and resolution fields. The custom-fields table supplies additional fields for some requests. Requests are counted once by ID, including those with no matching detail row. A closed request can represent a referral or other administrative action. These records alone do not verify a completed repair.

The archive comparison uses the week beginning April 1, 2025 and the latest complete Monday–Sunday week before the newest published request. It includes street-surface maintenance, right-of-way litter and graffiti categories. Dates and boundaries use the published UTC timestamps. Elapsed time uses valid date pairs. A populated resolution field means text was provided. Addresses, resolution text and other free text are omitted from this snapshot. HRM Open Data Licence.
